Hi,I'm Greta

This website is currently under construction, but scroll down for a quick summary of my interests and my skills! Thanks for stopping by!

While it's a bit nicer than my real view, this window shows the funky city of Sydney! This is where I'm currently living, working at Macquarie University as a teaching associate for the undergraduate Game Design and Development degree.

These are some game design and art books that I've collected over the years - my favourite one is Chaosium's Table Top Roleplaying Engine, which I use when I want to play around with designing ttrpg games, or even working on some crpg-style concepts. I also have a few art books designed to look like video game guides, which I regularly use for inspiration.

This is the little award I received from Ubisoft Sydney for completing their Develop With Ubisoft (Game Design Track) Mentorship Program! It was an excellent program that taught me a lot about working with large teams on even larger projects.

Here is the games project I'm currently working on, all boxed up and ready to ship (it's nice to have something to look forward to). At the moment, the project is Sunday Shift, a decorative resource management game about running your own op shop! A separate page dedicated to this project is coming soon.

This is my Game Design and Development degree from Macquarie University! It had an immense impact on me, not only on my approach to design, but also to how I consume and pick apart games as media. Other accredations I've collected are a number of GameDev.tv courses, and the Develop at Ubisoft design mentorship.

These are my passes from events such as PAX and South by Southwest. While I have so far only attended PAX as a visitor, I attended South by Southwest during its first ever run in Sydney as a presenter in the Student Showcase, for the project Aegis (see the 'My Projects' page).

This little screen shows Blender, inside this scene that I made in Blender! 3D art is where I'm directing a lot of energy, in terms of building up my skills and making sure that I am an effective jack-of-all-trades that can successfully develop solo projects. Much of my learning comes from GameDev.tv courses, which have been an incredible resource.

This screen shows Unity, the main game engine that I work in. Whilst it is the game engine that was taught throughout my degree, I have been branching out into Unreal Engine, and a bit of Twine for creative writing and planning more complex narrative games. All are phenomenal tools.

This is my cat! His name is Bossy, short for Big Boss, named after the Metal Gear character due to their shared shortage of eyes. That doesn't stop him though! He's become the unofficial mascot of Wonkyware, and provides me with inspiration every day. He may look sleepy here, but he is an energetic goofball, and a big fan of using his teeth to explore the world.

This isn't an exact replica of my keyboard, I just love the colour green. However, I do play the piano! It's the main instrument I use to write music, which I've done for both personal and professional projects (I'll have a songwriting credit on the to-be-released game Kindred Fates, thanks to a community competition). I also play bass guitar, which is useful both for music, and for making fun and weird effects to use in games.

This little shelf holds where it all began - a Nintendo DS Lite that I begged my parents for as a child. It came exclusively with gameboy games, with my favourite being Rayman Raving Rabbids. Of course, the collection has expanded a fair bit since then, but I still come back to it every now and again.
